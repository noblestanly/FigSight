About FigSight

FigSight is your smart companion for turning raw spreadsheets into clear, interactive insights. Whether you’re a business owner, analyst, or student, FigSight helps you make sense of data without the need for coding or complex tools.

Upload your Excel or CSV files and instantly get AI-powered filters, customizable charts, and interactive dashboards that let you explore your data in new ways.

Unlike traditional BI tools, FigSight is designed to be simple and lightweight. No steep learning curve, no expensive setup—just fast insights at your fingertips.
